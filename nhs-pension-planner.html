Saving beyond your NHS pension

Any SIPP, ISA, or other investments you're using to fill the gap your pensions don't cover — or to retire before your State Pension age.

SIPP contributions count toward your pension Annual Allowance (Step 6); ISA/GIA saving doesn't.
ISA withdrawals are tax-free — use 0% if it's all ISA. SIPP withdrawals are 25% tax-free then taxed as income — 10–20% is a reasonable blended estimate for most retirees; go higher if you expect to be a higher-rate taxpayer in retirement.

  • This models the 2015 NHS Pension Scheme (career average) only — it doesn't account for legacy 1995/2008-section service or the McCloud remedy, which affects many longer-serving members.
  • NHS pension and State Pension figures are simplified, real-terms (today's money) estimates — they are not a substitute for your NHS Pensions Total Reward Statement, a State Pension forecast, or regulated financial advice.
  • Early retirement reduction, Income Tax, National Insurance, and Annual Allowance figures are approximations using published rates and rules that change over time — always check current NHS Pensions and HMRC guidance.
  • Investment growth is never guaranteed. The Annual Allowance estimate ignores unused allowance carried forward from the previous three tax years, which could reduce or remove any charge shown.
